Program Identity

The three non-negotiables

Every player inside the Elite Raiders framework is trained through the same priorities: recover the ball quickly, control the pivot, and attack with purpose.

1. Press First
The team wins the ball back fast, with coordinated pressure and shared responsibility rather than isolated chasing.
Trigger: “PRESS!”
2. Control the Pivot Hub
The defensive midfielder acts as the connecting pillar between the back line and attacking phases.
Focus: scanning, switching, and tempo control
3. Attack Vertically
The attack must create fast, clear lanes to goal through wide overloads, direct combinations, and vertical support.
Outcome: more threat, less hesitation
